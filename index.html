About

Reads the room.
Holds the room.

DJ Play is Dawaun Lucas, a Compton-born DJ and MC who has been mixing since 2008. His sets pull from golden-era hip-hop, funk from the decades before it, R&B, throwbacks and current hits, so every generation in the room gets a song they know.

He's open format and works the mic too. That means smooth transitions, a dance floor that stays full, and an MC who keeps the night on schedule. He takes your must-play and do-not-play lists, and keeps it clean for church and family events.
